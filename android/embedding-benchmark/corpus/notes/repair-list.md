# Desk fan bearing repair notebook

The motor housing is silent at low speed and buzzes above half speed. The supply sits under the bench. Replacing foam around the feet reduced the sound transferred to the desk. A second unit with the same symptom had a slightly bent blade.

When the old bearing is removed, keep its shim and install it against the new bearing's inner race.

Finish by turning the blade by hand and confirming it clears the rear grille.
